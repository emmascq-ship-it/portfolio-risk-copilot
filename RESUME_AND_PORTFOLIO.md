# Resume and portfolio positioning

## Project title

**Multi-Asset Portfolio Risk Copilot**

Subtitle: **Python Risk Engine · Stress Testing · Evidence-Grounded AI Briefing**

## Resume bullets

- Developed an interactive Python risk platform calculating VaR, Expected Shortfall, beta, drawdown, factor sensitivities, risk contribution, and multi-asset scenario losses, with portfolio upload and automated limit monitoring.
- Implemented a 250-day rolling VaR backtest and controlled AI review workflow that converts validated results into evidence-linked findings and a draft risk brief while preserving human approval.

## Short LinkedIn description

Built an interactive and auditable portfolio-risk workflow that separates deterministic calculation from AI-assisted interpretation. The Python engine validates uploaded portfolio and market inputs, calculates VaR, Expected Shortfall, drawdown, beta, position-level risk contribution, factor sensitivities, and multi-asset stress losses, and monitors model performance through a rolling VaR backtest. A restricted evidence packet supports the daily risk brief while calculated metrics, limits, and final approval remain under analyst control.

## Portfolio-page structure

1. **Decision question:** Where is risk concentrated and what requires escalation?
2. **Risk snapshot:** NAV, volatility, VaR, Expected Shortfall, beta, and worst stress.
3. **Concentration:** Position weight versus contribution to portfolio volatility.
4. **Stress testing:** Equity, rates, credit, and USD scenarios.
5. **Model monitoring:** Rolling VaR estimates, exception rate, and exception log.
6. **Control design:** Data checks, calculation boundary, evidence packet, and human approval.
7. **Technical implementation:** Python, Streamlit, pandas, NumPy, SciPy, matplotlib, JSON, HTML, and optional LLM narration.
8. **Limitations:** Synthetic demo data, linear positions, no regulatory capital or derivatives.

## Interview positioning

This project supplies the quantitative-finance signal that is not visible from a general automation project. It connects Mathematics, FRM, Python, and risk communication without presenting an LLM as the source of calculated financial results.
